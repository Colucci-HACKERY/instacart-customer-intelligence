Dataset definitions and limitations

Monetary means purchase volume: the existing RFM calculation sums order_size per customer. These are product counts, not currency, prices or revenue. Null basket sizes are excluded from sums and averages; their contribution is unknown.

Recency is an existing relative proxy: the calculation sorts by order ID, skips the first row's interval and sums subsequent recorded gaps. It subtracts that total from the largest customer total. Order IDs do not establish chronology, and customer timelines have no shared calendar origin, so this is not verified days since the customer's last purchase. Stored scores and segments are preserved.

Day names use the platform's existing mapping (0 = Sunday through 6 = Saturday). A missing prior-order gap is shown as not recorded; zero remains zero. No dates or spending values are inferred.
