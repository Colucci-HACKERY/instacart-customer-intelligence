How to read these numbers

Containing orders counts distinct order IDs containing a product, aisle or department. Occurrences counts source order-product rows, not quantities purchased. Duplicate rows would increase occurrences but not containing orders; this importer does not silently discard them. Category order counts are calculated directly and are not the sum of product order counts.

Reorder count counts rows where reordered = 1. Reorder rate is that count divided by rows with a valid 0 or 1 indicator, multiplied by 100. It is not the share of customers who repurchase. Unknown indicators are excluded from the denominator; a zero denominator displays Unknown. Rates with small denominators can rank highly.

Scope: the original order_products__prior.csv joined to products.csv, aisles.csv and departments.csv. Train/test data is excluded. Raw rows remain in the source files; this dashboard reads an imported aggregate snapshot. The existing customer dataset drops first orders and retains later train/test orders; equal overall order counts do not imply matching order IDs. Product summaries therefore use the original prior-order population independently of the processed customer records and stored RFM values. No prices, revenue or profit are available.
