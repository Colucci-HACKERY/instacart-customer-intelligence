Historical behaviour, with clear limits.

Sequence comes from order_number.

Original source orders are ordered within each customer by order_number. Order IDs identify records; their numerical value never establishes chronology. Duplicate, missing or invalid sequence numbers quarantine that customer's entire sequence from gap metrics.

Measure completed gaps.

Mean and median use valid integer observations from 0 to 30 for orders after the first. Zero is a same-day gap. Missing, negative, nonfinite, fractional or out-of-range gaps are excluded, with their counts shown. 30 means 30+; capped averages are lower bounds on uncapped averages.

Do not infer current churn.

No calendar dates, common observation cutoff or post-final-order follow-up are available. Day-of-week and hour fields cannot supply dates. Neither these gaps nor stored RFM recency establish current inactivity, churn probability, calendar cohort retention or customer lifetime value.

Scope, comparisons and preservation

Shares within 7 and 14 days divide qualifying completed gaps by all valid recorded completed gaps, not by customers or eligible gap slots. Missing observations may affect representativeness. Earlier means use available valid non-first-order gaps strictly before the highest order_number; latest gaps are never substituted with an earlier observation when unavailable. A directional comparison uses only uncapped observations and requires at least two earlier valid gaps. These are descriptions, not risk scores or predictions.

Original orders.csv includes first orders and final train/test order metadata. The existing processed customer dataset drops first orders; Product and Basket Intelligence use prior-order contents. These populations are not interchangeable. The dataset contains selected observed customer histories and does not establish a representative business retention rate. No transaction dates, prices, revenue or profit are inferred.

The streaming builder writes only a separate versioned cadence artifact. Existing orders, RFM scores and segments, product summaries and basket snapshots are not recalculated or modified. Current RFM labels are joined read-only at request time. Rebuild explicitly when the original source changes.
